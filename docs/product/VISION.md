# Product Vision

## Vision statement
Make farm operations, field-level collaboration, farmer recruitment, and agricultural decision support more organized and traceable through one accessible digital platform.

## Intended users
- Small and medium farm owners
- Agricultural workers/farmers
- Users exploring agricultural work opportunities

## Value
- Centralized farm and field records
- More transparent recruitment and assignment workflows
- Farmer-specific workspace
- Traceable crop and soil observations
- AI-assisted insights with clear limitations

## Product principles
1. Owner control with explicit permissions.
2. Farmer access limited to assigned work.
3. Historical traceability.
4. Explainable and cautious AI assistance.
5. Responsive, accessible user experience.
6. Secure-by-default data handling.
